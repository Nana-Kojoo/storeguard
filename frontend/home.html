What's inside

Everything a busy shop needs, nothing it doesn't

Built for owners who can't be on the shop floor all day, and for cashiers who need a till that never gets in the way.

  • A fast, forgiving point of sale

    Scan barcodes or tap product tiles, hold a sale while a customer fetches something, apply discounts within the limit you set, and print a clean receipt on 58mm, 80mm or A4 paper.

  • Inventory & categories

    Stock levels, reorder alerts, expiry dates and a record of every stock movement.

  • Shifts & cash drawers

    Cashiers open with a counted float and close with a denomination count. Shortages are flagged.

  • Refunds with approval

    Refunds, big expenses and cash differences go to a manager. Nobody approves their own.

  • Loss prevention

    Alerts for unusual refunds, large discounts, voids and shortages, plus a full audit log.

  • Suppliers & purchases

    Raise purchase orders and receive deliveries straight into stock with real costs.

  • Customers on credit

    Keep customer records with credit limits and balances, record repayments and see who owes what.

  • Staff & permissions

    Six roles, with permissions you can fine-tune for each person.

  • Ready for bad connections

    Sales made during an outage are saved on the till and synced once, automatically.

Get StoreGuard

Install StoreGuard, step by step

There are three ways to run StoreGuard, and they're all the same software. Most shops install the app on each till; shops with unreliable internet run it on a store PC.

Install as an app Recommended for tills

StoreGuard installs straight from this website, with no app store needed. It gets its own icon and window, starts quickly and updates itself.

  • Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Android, iPhone and iPad
  • Opens from the desktop, taskbar or home screen
  • Keeps your product list on the till

Windows PC, Mac or Chromebook

Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

  1. Open this website in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Install StoreGuard above. If no window pops up, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install to confirm.
  4. StoreGuard opens in its own window. Right-click its taskbar icon and choose Pin to taskbar.
  5. Sign in with your StoreGuard email and password.

Android phone or tablet

Use Google Chrome.

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install StoreGuard above, or tap the menu ⋮ and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install.
  4. Open StoreGuard from your home screen and sign in.

iPhone or iPad

Use Safari.

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button .
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open StoreGuard from your home screen and sign in.

Sign each cashier in once while the till is online. From then on the till can open StoreGuard and keep selling through an internet outage. Sales sync automatically when the connection returns.

Run it on your store PC No internet needed day to day

Download StoreGuard and run it on a Windows PC in your shop. That PC becomes your store's own server: your data stays in your shop, and the tills connect to it over the shop Wi-Fi.

Download for Windows (.zip)

Windows 10 or 11 · about 1 MB · free to download

  • Double-click to start, no technical setup
  • Automatic daily backups, last 30 kept
  • Other tills join over the shop network

Set up the store PC

  1. Download the zip file with the button above.
  2. Extract it. Right-click the file in Downloads, choose Extract All…, and extract it to a permanent place such as C:\StoreGuard. Don't put it in Downloads or a OneDrive or Dropbox folder.
  3. Start it. Open the folder and double-click Start StoreGuard. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Let it finish setting up. The first start needs the internet once and takes 1–2 minutes. If Python is missing, the window offers to install it. Press Y, wait, then double-click Start StoreGuard again.
  5. Allow the firewall. If Windows Firewall asks, tick Private networks and click Allow, so the other tills can connect.
  6. Create your business. Your browser opens StoreGuard. Choose Create business and register the owner account.
  7. Make it an app. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar to give StoreGuard a desktop icon on this PC.
  8. Keep the black StoreGuard window open while the shop is trading. Closing it stops StoreGuard for every till.

Connect the other tills, tablets and phones

  1. Connect the device to the same Wi-Fi or network as the store PC.
  2. Look in the StoreGuard window on the store PC for the line Other tills/tablets: http://192.168.…:8000.
  3. Type that address into the device's browser and sign in. Bookmark it, or add it to the home screen.

Good habits

  • Start automatically: press Windows+R, type shell:startup, press Enter, and put a shortcut to Start StoreGuard in the folder that opens.
  • Back up weekly: copy the backups folder to a USB drive or cloud storage.
  • Lock new sign-ups: after creating your business, open .env in Notepad and change ALLOW_REGISTRATION=true to false.
  • Full details, including restoring a backup and updating, are in READ ME FIRST inside the download.

How to use StoreGuard

Operating StoreGuard, step by step

From the owner's first set-up to a cashier's daily routine. Each guide follows the order you'll actually do things in the shop.

First-time setup Owner · about 20 minutes

  1. Create your business. On the sign-in page choose Create business. The account you create is the owner account, so use an email you check and a strong password.
  2. Fill in Settings. Open Settings from the side menu. Enter your shop's name, branch, address and phone, which print on every receipt, then the currency code (for example GHS), tax rate and time zone.
  3. Set up receipts. Still in Settings, upload your logo, add a footer and returns policy, choose your paper size (58mm, 80mm or A4) and decide whether receipts print automatically.
  4. Set your limits. Choose the largest discount a cashier may give (Max cashier discount) and the loss-prevention thresholds that raise security alerts. Leave Cashiers must open a shift before selling switched on.
  5. Add your products in Inventory, in one of three ways:
    • Quick add: type many products into a grid, one per row.
    • Import spreadsheet: upload your existing Excel or CSV list. StoreGuard checks it and shows any problems before importing.
    • Add product: one at a time, with a photo, barcode, cost price, selling price, reorder level and expiry date.
  6. Add your suppliers in Suppliers & Purchases, so deliveries can be received into stock.
  7. Add your staff in Employees. Give each person their own account and the right role (see Who can do what). Never share logins, because every action is recorded against the person who did it.
  8. Install StoreGuard on each till (see Install), name each till (for example “Till 1”), and sign every cashier in once while online.
  9. Do a test sale and a test refund, print a receipt, then close the test shift. You're ready to trade.

Opening the shop Cashier · every shift

  1. Sign in on your till with your own email and password.
  2. Open Point of Sale. If you have no open shift, StoreGuard asks you to open one.
  3. Count the float. Count the cash already in the drawer and enter the exact amount. Choose your till name if asked.
  4. Click Open shift. Your name and the time are recorded, and you're ready to sell.
  5. Check the printer has paper and the barcode scanner beeps when you scan an item.

Supervisors: open Shifts & Attendance to see who has started, who is late and which tills are open.

Making a sale Cashier

  1. Add items. Scan the barcode, or press F2, type part of the name or SKU and press Enter. You can also tap a product tile. Scan again to add more of the same item.
  2. Adjust the cart. Use + and − to change quantities, or × to remove a line. If your role allows discounts, click edit next to Discount; amounts over the owner's limit are blocked.
  3. Regular customer? Attach them to the sale so it shows on their record in Customers & Credit.
  4. Customer waiting? Click Hold to park the sale and serve the next person. Resume it later from Held.
  5. Charge. Under the cart, choose how the customer pays, then click Charge or press F9:
    • Cash: enter the amount handed over. StoreGuard shows the change to give.
    • Mobile Money: choose the network (MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo) and enter the transaction ID from the customer's message.
    • Card: take the payment on your card machine, then enter its reference.
  6. Click Payment received (cash: Complete sale). The receipt appears and prints. Need another copy? Click Last receipt at the top of the till.

Internet down? Keep selling. An Offline badge shows at the top and each sale is saved on the till. When the connection returns, the sales upload by themselves. Don't clear the browser's data while sales are waiting to sync.

Refunds & approvals Cashier & manager

  1. Open Sales & Transactions (cashiers see My Transactions) and find the sale. Search by receipt number or filter by date.
  2. Open the sale and click Refund…. Choose the items and quantities being returned and write the reason. Amounts are adjusted automatically for any discount and tax on the original sale.
  3. Click Request refund. Cashiers' refunds go to Approvals, and the cashier can follow them in My Refunds. Supervisors and managers can process a refund straight away.
  4. Manager or supervisor: open Approvals, check the reason and the original sale, then Approve or Reject. You can't approve your own request.
  5. Once a refund is approved, give the customer their money. Returned items go back into stock automatically.

Large expenses and cash-drawer differences also come to Approvals. Unusual patterns, such as many refunds from one cashier, raise an alert in Security & Alerts.

Stock & deliveries Stock clerk & manager

  1. Watch for low stock. The Overview and Inventory pages flag products at or below their reorder level.
  2. Order. In Suppliers & Purchases, create a purchase order, pick the supplier and add the products and quantities.
  3. Receive the delivery. When the goods arrive, open the purchase order, check it against the delivery note and click Mark as received. Stock levels and cost prices update immediately.
  4. Fix a count. After a stock take, click Adjust stock on the product in Inventory and choose the reason, such as damaged, expired or a count correction. Every change is logged in Stock Movements.
  5. Update prices by editing the product. The new price reaches every till as soon as it's saved.

Closing up Cashier & manager · every shift

  1. Finish or clear any held sales.
  2. Open My Cash Drawer and find Count & close drawer.
  3. Count the cash by denomination: enter how many of each note and coin you have. StoreGuard adds it up and compares it with what the drawer should hold.
  4. If the count is short or over, explain why in Notes for your supervisor. The difference goes to a manager for review.
  5. Click Close drawer & end shift, then sign out.
  6. Manager: check Cash Management and Approvals for flagged drawers, then glance at Security & Alerts.

Reports & oversight Owner & manager

  1. Overview shows today's revenue, profit, sales count, low stock and open alerts at a glance. It's the first page owners see.
  2. Reports & Analytics breaks the numbers down by day, week, month or quarter: best sellers, payment mix, staff performance and margins. Click Export for a CSV you can open in Excel.
  3. Security & Alerts lists unusual refunds, big discounts, voids and cash shortages. Open each one, check it and mark it resolved.
  4. Audit Logs show who did what and when, including price changes, stock adjustments and sign-ins.
  5. Device Management lists the tills and phones that use the point of sale. Rename them, or block or remove one that's lost or no longer used.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need the internet to use StoreGuard?

Not to sell. Once a till has signed in, it keeps selling through outages and syncs afterwards. If you run StoreGuard on your store PC, the shop doesn't need the internet at all, except once for the first-time setup.

What equipment do I need?

Any Windows PC, laptop, Mac, Chromebook, Android tablet or iPad with a modern browser. USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners that “type” the code work out of the box. Receipts print to any printer your device can use, including 58mm and 80mm thermal receipt printers.

Can I have several tills?

Yes. Install StoreGuard on each till and give every cashier their own login. With the store PC option, the other tills connect over your shop network using the address shown in the StoreGuard window.

Does it handle Mobile Money?

Yes. Choose Mobile Money at checkout, pick the network (MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo) and record the transaction ID. It appears on the receipt and in your payment reports.

How is my data kept safe?

Each person has their own login with only the permissions their role needs. Accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords, every sensitive action goes into an audit log, and the app is protected against common web attacks. With the store PC option your data never leaves your shop, and a backup is taken automatically every day.

How do I back up and restore?

On the store PC, StoreGuard saves a dated copy of your database in the backups folder every day and keeps the last 30. Copy that folder to a USB drive or cloud storage regularly. “READ ME FIRST” in the download explains how to restore one.

Troubleshooting

Something not working?

I forgot my password

Ask your owner or manager to reset it in Employees. Owners who are locked out should contact support below.

“Account locked” after wrong passwords

For your security, the account locks for 15 minutes after repeated wrong passwords. Wait for the time shown and try again. If you've forgotten the password, ask a manager to reset it in Employees.

The till says “Offline” or sales are waiting to sync

Check the till's Wi-Fi or network cable. If you use a store PC, make sure its black StoreGuard window is open. Sales upload by themselves once the connection is back; leave the till on and don't clear its browser data.

Other tills can't reach the store PC

Make sure they're on the same Wi-Fi as the store PC and are using the exact address shown in its StoreGuard window. If it still fails, open Windows Security → Firewall → Allow an app on the store PC and allow Python on Private networks.

The receipt doesn't print or looks cut off

Check the printer is on and set as the default printer. In Settings → Receipts, choose the paper size that matches your printer (58mm or 80mm). In the print window, set margins to None and scale to 100%.

The barcode scanner does nothing

Click inside the search box (or press F2) and scan again. Test the scanner in Notepad: if no numbers appear there, it isn't connected or charged.

The “Install” button doesn't appear

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. Installing also needs a secure https:// address, or http://localhost:8000 on the store PC itself.
